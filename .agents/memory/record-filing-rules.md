---
name: Record Filing rules
description: DPU scope and the two authoritative modern filing handoffs.
---

Record Filing belongs under Business Tracking and is for the DPU team, with
existing administrator oversight. Do not give DPU broad access to other
business workflows just to make filing documents accessible.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a page similar to legacy
Business_Tracking_Filing.aspx, under Business Tracking, accessed by DPU.

**How to apply:** Enforce the same role on queue, detail, completion and document
downloads. Staff enrollment is separate from creating the profile; never
silently reassign existing users.

Receive eligible business after receipting **or** after the Closed action in
Allocation Files, across all supported business types including endorsements.
Do not require legacy policy-stage flags for either route.

**Why:** The user corrected the routing to these two sources. Cashier marks
fully paid files receipted but not partial receipts, and Allocation Files' Closed
action is an alternative even without a receipt. The user requested this without
a New/Renewal/Addon-only exception. The legacy Filing query did
require completed policy stages; that remains documented as legacy behavior,
not a modern prerequisite.

**How to apply:** Keep the role and active/nondeclined/not-filed checks; accept
records marked receipted or CLOSED by Allocation Files. Never set policy-stage
flags true merely to populate this queue. Document selection is optional:
filing a record does not mean every upload was selected. Keep notes append-only,
individual document filing metadata, archived history and atomic completion;
notification queued is not email sent.

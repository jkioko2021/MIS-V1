---
name: Closed-action risk reassessment
description: User-approved completion policy after returning or reopening a department register.
---
Every active risk must be explicitly reassessed after Return or Reopen, including risks whose root actions are all closed. Those risks must remain available in Update Register and reappear in the saved summary after assessment.

**Why:** The user chose reassessment of every active risk rather than carrying forward completed closed-action assessments.

**How to apply:** Root-action closure is independent of register assessment completion. Do not exempt or auto-assess closed-action risks, reopen their causes, refresh historical rankings on return/reopen, or remove history. Preserve the existing explicit assessment-save and ranking-refresh boundaries.

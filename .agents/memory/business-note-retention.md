---
name: Business note retention
description: User-required retention of business tracking and review notes.
---

Saved business notes must not be deleted or hidden. Queue moves and review decisions must retain the original notes with the same business file; corrections should be recorded as new notes.

**Why:** The user explicitly stated, “notes should not be deleted.”

**How to apply:** Do not introduce note-delete controls or endpoints, soft-deactivation, replacement of prior notes, or note cleanup during business handoffs. Clearing a submitted input is acceptable only after its note has been persisted in the file history.
